Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Softphonic Technologies Private Limited ("Softphonic", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our website at softphonic.com (the "Website"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and being transparent about how this Website works.

Information we collect

This Website is a static informational website. It does not include user accounts, databases, or server-side processing. We do not automatically collect personal information through forms on this Website.

The enquiry form on our Contact page does not transmit data to any server. Instead, it prepares a message in your own email application using the details you enter, which you then choose to send to us. Any information you include in that email is shared with us voluntarily.

Information you share with us directly

When you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp, or social media, you may share information such as your name, company name, email address, phone number, and details about your enquiry. We use this information solely to respond to you and to discuss potential or ongoing work.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • To provide, discuss, and deliver our services.
  • To maintain records of our correspondence and engagements.

Third-party services

This Website loads web fonts from a third-party font provider to display content. When your browser requests these fonts, the provider may receive standard technical information such as your IP address. We do not control how third-party providers process such information.

Links to external platforms (such as LinkedIn, Instagram, or WhatsApp) are governed by the privacy policies of those respective services.

Cookies

This Website does not set its own tracking cookies. Any cookies that may be set are limited to those required by third-party services you choose to interact with.

Data security

Because this Website does not store personal data, the primary information we hold is the correspondence you send us directly. We take reasonable measures to keep that information secure and to limit access to it.

Your choices

You can choose not to contact us or share personal information. If you have contacted us and would like us to update or delete the information you shared, please reach out using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
